# VastGame 0.5.22 beta

- **Share diagnostics from VastGame.** Open **Session diagnostics** next to the streaming controls or on the startup and finish screens. Review the report before copying it to send to support.
- The report shows the last known stage of the rental, connection, game launch, or cloud sync. It includes Moonlight and Steam Link launch and pairing results and can identify known Steam startup errors on the rented machine.
- The exported report uses fixed status and error codes. Raw log lines, account names, IP addresses, tokens, and game paths stay out of the report.
